# Licensing

REDZ.UI has two licenses, one for each part.

| Part | License | What it allows |
|---|---|---|
| The library: `css/`, `js/`, `charts/`, `calendar/`, `media/`, `docs/`, `skills/`, `tools/` and their files in `dist/` | [MIT](LICENSE) | Everything the MIT License allows, in any project |
| The editor: `editor/` and `dist/js/redz-ui-editor.min.js`, `dist/css/redz-ui-editor.min.css`, `dist/css/redz-ui-editor-surface.min.css` | [PolyForm Strict 1.0.0](editor/LICENSE) | Noncommercial use only, as it is. No commercial use, no changes, no redistribution |

**Versions.** The editor is under PolyForm Strict from 0.17.0. Versions up to and including 0.16.0 stay under MIT, as they were released.

**Commercial use of the editor.** It comes with REDZ products, under their terms. For any other commercial use, or to change or redistribute it, ask for a separate license: open an issue at https://github.com/redz-tech/REDZ.UI.

**The name.** Neither license gives any right to the name REDZ or REDZ.UI, or to any logo. A project built on the library may say so, and may not call itself by these names.

**Other projects.** Material from other projects keeps its own license, listed in [NOTICE](NOTICE).

The texts in `LICENSE` and `editor/LICENSE` are the licenses; this page only explains them.
