App shell

The frame of an admin screen: app with a header, a side navigation, the main column and an optional side column. Below 1024 px the side navigation becomes a panel opened from the header. These docs stand in it. A site's header is a navbar.

The parts

The shell is the one part of the library that looks at the window; everything inside it measures its own region. app-main and app-aside are regions, so md: inside the main column measures the column.

<div class="app">
  <header class="app-header">
    <button class="btn btn-text btn-icon app-burger" type="button" commandfor="side" command="toggle-popover" aria-label="Sections">…</button>
    <a class="app-brand" href="/"><span class="app-mark">RZ</span>REDZ CMS</a>
    <button class="btn app-search" type="button" aria-keyshortcuts="Meta+K">…</button>
  </header>
  <nav id="side" class="app-sidebar" popover aria-label="Sections">
    <div class="app-section">Content</div>
    <a class="app-link" href="/excursions/" aria-current="page">…Excursions</a>
  </nav>
  <main class="app-main" id="main">…</main>
  <aside class="app-aside">…</aside>
</div>

The demo screens show the whole shell: a list, a resource page with its side column, a settings form.

Folded to a rail

On a wide window the side navigation folds into a rail of its icons and back: a header button with command="--fold" and aria-controls naming the sidebar puts data-folded on it and says so in aria-pressed. Folded, each link shows its words as a tooltip and keeps them as its name. The library stores nothing: it tells the page in redz:fold (detail.folded), and the page that keeps the choice writes data-folded back on the sidebar when it loads. These docs fold so - the button beside their burger.

<button class="btn btn-text btn-icon" type="button" command="--fold" aria-controls="side" aria-label="Fold the sections">…</button>

document.addEventListener('redz:fold', (event) => localStorage.setItem('folded', event.detail.folded ? '1' : ''));

A shell without app-sidebar - a page whose role has no sections - is one column by itself: no class to add.

A banner for the whole system

A state of the whole system - a pause for maintenance, a lost connection - shows in app-banner: the first row of the shell, above its header, across the window, sticky with the header under it. REDZ.ui.banner() shows it and answers a function that removes it; REDZ.ui.banner(null) removes it too. There is one at a time: a later call replaces it, and the same tone and words again change nothing, so a page may call it on every answer of its server. Its words are said once. On a page without the shell it goes first in the body.

REDZ.ui.banner({tone: 'warning', text: 'The system is paused for maintenance.'});
REDZ.ui.banner(null);

The tones are app-banner-info, the default, app-banner-success, app-banner-warning and app-banner-danger. The banner's height, which grows as its words wrap, goes to --redz-app-banner-height on the root: the header, the side navigation, a table's head and the scroll padding stand under it. A banner written in the page's markup in its place is measured the same.

A new version is ready. It loads with the next page.
The system is back. Every change is saved.
The system is paused for maintenance. Your changes are kept; try again in a few minutes.
The connection is lost. Changes made now are not saved.

A bar at the bottom

action-bar holds what a long screen is for - a total and Pay, a count and Save - at the bottom of the window while the page scrolls under it: sticky, across its region on the page's own tinted floor, a line above it. As the last child of app-main it reaches the region's edges. Its padding keeps clear of a phone's home bar and rounded corners - the page asks for that room with viewport-fit=cover in its viewport meta - and on a touch screen it steps aside while a field has the focus, as the keyboard takes its place. action-bar-text is its words, which take the room left. It stays off paper.

Keyboard and accessibility

  • The header is a <header>, the side navigation a named <nav>, the main column the page's one <main>.
  • On a narrow screen the burger opens the navigation as a popover: Esc or a click outside closes it, and the focus goes back to the burger.
  • The current section is aria-current="page", drawn as a bar that stays in forced colors.
  • The fold button is a toggle button, pressed while the rail is folded; it has no commandfor, which WebKit would announce as collapsed. On a narrow screen it hides, as the burger opens the whole navigation there.
  • The banner's words are said once as it shows - politely, at once for danger - and not again for the same call; a banner in the markup is read in its place, first on the page.
  • The banner, the header and the navigation stay off paper: only the main column prints.
