App shell

The frame of an admin screen: app with a header, a side navigation, the main column and an optional side column. Below 1024 px the side navigation becomes a panel opened from the header. These docs stand in it. A site's header is a navbar.

The parts

The shell is the one part of the library that looks at the window; everything inside it measures its own region. app-main and app-aside are regions, so md: inside the main column measures the column.

<div class="app">
  <header class="app-header">
    <button class="btn btn-text btn-icon app-burger" type="button" commandfor="side" command="toggle-popover" aria-label="Sections">…</button>
    <a class="app-brand" href="/"><span class="app-mark" aria-hidden="true">RZ</span><span class="app-name">REDZ CMS</span></a>
    <button class="btn app-search" type="button" aria-keyshortcuts="Meta+K">…</button>
  </header>
  <nav id="side" class="app-sidebar" popover aria-label="Sections">
    <a class="app-link" href="/">…Overview</a>
    <div class="app-section">Content</div>
    <a class="app-link" href="/excursions/" aria-current="page">…Excursions</a>
    <details class="app-group" id="side-sales">
      <summary class="app-section">Sales</summary>
      <a class="app-link" href="/bookings/">…Bookings</a>
    </details>
  </nav>
  <main class="app-main" id="main">
    <div class="grid lg:cols-12 gap-24 align-items-start">
      <div class="lg:span-8 vstack gap-16">…</div>
      <aside class="app-aside lg:span-4" aria-label="About this excursion">…</aside>
    </div>
  </main>
</div>

The side column app-aside stands in the main column's grid, beside its content, and goes under it where the column is narrow; the shell's own grid has no place for it.

The demo screens show the whole shell: a list, a resource page with its side column, a settings form.

The brand

app-brand is the link home at the header's start: the product's mark, app-mark, and its name, app-name. The mark is a picture of the name, so it carries aria-hidden="true". Where the header is under 576 px wide, the brand is its mark alone. The name leaves the sight but stays the link's name for a screen reader.

A product with its own logo puts it in place of the name: <img class="brand-logo">, the picture for the light theme, then <img class="brand-logo brand-logo-dark">, its twin for the dark one. Each carries the file's own width and height: the logo stands 28 px tall, as wide as its proportions make it, and nothing moves as it loads. In the header it is at most 160 px wide.

  • The page's theme picks the picture: data-theme on html, or the system's without it. A region's own data-theme leaves the logo as it is.
  • A light picture with no twin stands on a white mat in the dark theme.
  • With the mark printed too, the header under 576 px shows the mark and keeps the logo's alt as the link's name. Without the mark, the logo narrows where the header is full, its picture kept whole.
  • The alt of both pictures is the product's name; only the one shown is read.

A credit line

<footer class="app-credit"> after app-main, in app itself, is a quiet line under the main column: small, muted, its link in the line's colour and underlined. The words are the page's. On a short page it stands at the window's foot; on a long one after the column's end. It is the page's contentinfo, outside main, so a save bar in the column never covers it. Print leaves it out.

<div class="app">
  <header class="app-header">…</header>
  <nav class="app-sidebar" …>…</nav>
  <main class="app-main">…</main>
  <footer class="app-credit">Powered by <a href="https://example.com/">Example CMS</a></footer>
</div>

Folded to a rail

On a wide window the side navigation folds into a rail of its icons and back: a header button with command="--fold" and aria-controls naming the sidebar puts data-folded on it and says so in aria-pressed. Folded, each link shows its words as a tooltip and keeps them as its name. The library stores nothing: it tells the page in redz:fold (detail.folded), and the page that keeps the choice writes data-folded back on the sidebar when it loads. These docs fold so - the button beside their burger.

<button class="btn btn-text btn-icon" type="button" command="--fold" aria-controls="side" aria-label="Fold the sections">…</button>

document.addEventListener('redz:fold', (event) => localStorage.setItem('folded', event.detail.folded ? '1' : ''));

A shell without app-sidebar - a page whose role has no sections - is one column by itself: no class to add.

Groups that open and close

A section of the side navigation may open and close: <details class="app-group"> with its <summary class="app-section">, the chevron drawn by the library. The server prints each group open or closed as the person left it, so the navigation stands right before any script and without one. The group that holds the current page - its aria-current="page" link - opens as the page loads, even after the person closed it. A person's open or close is told in redz:group (detail.open) on the group, which the page keeps by the group's id; the library stores nothing, and its own opening is not told. A plain div.app-section still heads a section that does not close. These docs' sections are groups, kept in this browser.

<details class="app-group" id="side-sales" open>
  <summary class="app-section">Sales</summary>
  <a class="app-link" href="/bookings/">…Bookings</a>
</details>

document.addEventListener('redz:group', (event) => save(event.target.id, event.detail.open));

Folded to a rail, every group opens, as its icons are the way in, and a line stands between two groups or sections; as the rail unfolds, the groups it opened close again.

Changing pages

From one page of a site to the next the header and the side navigation stand still and the rest cross-fades, with no white flash between them - in Chrome, Edge and Safari 18.2 and later; Firefox changes pages as before. data-transition on <html>, which the server prints from the setting, chooses:

  • "fade" - the default, the same as no attribute: the new page fades in.
  • "instant" - the new page stands at once, still with no flash.
  • "busy" - while the server prepares the next page, the main column lies under the busy overlay with its spinner, from 400 ms on, and the new page stands at once.
  • "none" - no transition: a page changes as it always did.

For people who ask for less motion nothing fades and nothing moves; "busy" keeps its overlay, the spinner turning slower.

<html lang="en" data-transition="busy">

The overlay of "busy" comes where the browser has the Navigation API - Chrome, Edge, Firefox 147, Safari 26.2 - for a navigation to another page of the site. Not for a click with a key or the middle button, a link to another tab, window or frame, a download link, a link to a fragment of this page, or a navigation a handler prevents or takes over. A link whose answer is a file carries download: nothing tells a page that a navigation became a download, so the overlay would lift only by itself, after ten seconds. Escape lifts it at once, and so does coming back to the page from the browser's cache. data-busy on app-main gives the words under the spinner, and a busy-art first in it shows the page's own picture in its place - see Busy.

Where the side navigation stands

A long side navigation is placed as the page loads, and as its panel opens on a narrow screen, by data-scroll on app-sidebar:

  • "keep" - where the person left it in this tab, kept in the tab's sessionStorage; the top when the storage is empty or refuses. It is the one thing the library stores, and only where the page asks.
  • "current" - the current page's link brought into view.
  • No attribute - the top.
<nav id="side" class="app-sidebar" popover aria-label="Sections" data-scroll="keep">

A banner for the whole system

A state of the whole system - a pause for maintenance, a lost connection - shows in app-banner: the first row of the shell, above its header, across the window, sticky with the header under it. REDZ.ui.banner() shows it and answers a function that removes it; REDZ.ui.banner(null) removes it too. There is one at a time: a later call replaces it, and the same tone and words again change nothing, so a page may call it on every answer of its server. Its words are said once. On a page without the shell it goes first in the body.

REDZ.ui.banner({tone: 'warning', text: 'The system is paused for maintenance.'});
REDZ.ui.banner(null);

The tones are app-banner-info, the default, app-banner-success, app-banner-warning and app-banner-danger. The banner's height, which grows as its words wrap, goes to --redz-app-banner-height on the root: the header, the side navigation, a table's head and the scroll padding stand under it. A banner written in the page's markup in its place is measured the same.

A new version is ready. It loads with the next page.
The system is back. Every change is saved.
The system is paused for maintenance. Your changes are kept; try again in a few minutes.
The connection is lost. Changes made now are not saved.

A bar at the bottom

action-bar holds what a long screen is for - a total and Pay, a count and Save - at the bottom of the window while the page scrolls under it: sticky, across its region on the page's own tinted floor, a line above it. As the last child of app-main it reaches the region's edges. Its padding keeps clear of a phone's home bar and rounded corners - the page asks for that room with viewport-fit=cover in its viewport meta - and on a touch screen it steps aside while a field has the focus, as the keyboard takes its place. action-bar-text is its words, which take the room left. It stays off paper.

Keyboard and accessibility

  • The header is a <header>, the side navigation a named <nav>, the main column the page's one <main>.
  • On a narrow screen the burger opens the navigation as a popover: Esc or a click outside closes it, and the focus goes back to the burger.
  • The current section is aria-current="page", drawn as a bar that stays in forced colors.
  • The fold button is a toggle button, pressed while the rail is folded; it has no commandfor, which WebKit would announce as collapsed. On a narrow screen it hides, as the burger opens the whole navigation there.
  • A group's heading is the browser's own disclosure: Enter or Space opens and closes it, and a screen reader says whether it is expanded. In the rail the headings leave the page and its tab order, as every link stands there with its name.
  • The page that changes is announced by the browser as always; under "busy" the main column turns inert, and one polite message says it is loading once the overlay shows.
  • The banner's words are said once as it shows - politely, at once for danger - and not again for the same call; a banner in the markup is read in its place, first on the page.
  • The banner, the header and the navigation stay off paper: only the main column prints.
