Badges

A badge is a short label of state or count beside the thing it describes: Published, Draft, 5 new. It never acts — a badge that does something is a chip or a button.

Examples

The plain badge is neutral, for a status that needs no attention. badge-subtle-{tone} is the usual colored form; badge-{tone}, filled, is for the one that must be seen. An icon before the words carries the meaning in forced colors and for people who do not tell the colors apart.

Accessibility

  • A badge's words are read with the thing it stands beside; write them so — "5" in "New 5" reads as five new orders only if the link says New.
  • The tones of text against their fill are at least 4.5:1 in both themes; a tone alone is never the meaning.
