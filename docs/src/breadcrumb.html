Breadcrumbs

The way up from a record to the list it belongs to, above the record's title. The separators are drawn by CSS and turn with the direction.

Examples

  1. Content
  2. Excursions
  3. Ang Thong

Accessibility

  • A <nav aria-label="Breadcrumb"> around an ordered list; the last item is the page itself, aria-current="page", not a link.
  • The separators are not read: they are drawn, not written.
