Busy

REDZ.ui.busy(element, promise) marks a button or a region busy until the promise settles: a button shows a spinner and ignores clicks, a region turns inert under an overlay.

Examples

A region - a card, its body, a dialog's or a drawer's body, any region - keeps its old content under a dimmed overlay while the new comes. The overlay shows only after 400 ms, so a quick answer does not flash; once shown it stays at least 400 ms. A focus the region dropped comes back to the same element, to the element with its id in the new content, or to the region.

A button

The button keeps its focus, ignores clicks until the promise settles and shows a spinner: in place of its leading icon, or over its words when it has none - then it keeps its size too. data-busy on the button gives the words it shows while it waits, said politely once the wait passes 400 ms; set it again before each call when the wait has steps.

An item of a menu - Delete in a row's menu - is busy with the button that opened the menu: the menu closes as the item is pressed, and the opener shows the spinner in its place and swallows its clicks until the promise settles. In a menu inside a menu, the outermost opener does.

Any other element - a cell whose check runs, a line - shows a small ring after 400 ms and, to a screen reader, the words of its data-busy or the page's text busy, saying nothing aloud: many such checks at once would be noise.

Accessibility

  • Screen readers mostly ignore aria-busy, and on a live region it breaks the region in some of them: here it is a styling hook, and busy() refuses a live region in the console.
  • The region is inert while busy: no focus, no clicks, out of the accessibility tree. When the overlay shows, one polite message says so - the page's text busy, “Loading…”.
  • For a first load, where there is nothing to cover yet, use placeholders.
