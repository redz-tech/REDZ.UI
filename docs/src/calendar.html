Calendars

A month of days with what happens on each, a strip of days across the things that can be busy on them - equipment, rooms, boats -, and months to pick a day, a period or several days from, by the page's own rules and settings.

A package of the release

Calendars are linked apart, on the screens that show them, beside the two files. A month or a strip needs no script; picking a date needs the package's script too, after the library's.

<link rel="stylesheet" href="redz-ui/css/redz-ui-calendar.min.css">
<script type="module" src="redz-ui/js/redz-ui-calendar.min.js"></script>

A month

table class="calendar": a weekday header, the week starting on the day the page chooses, then six weeks of days. A day holds its time class="calendar-day", its events - calendar-event, a dot of a tone and the words, cut to the width, the whole in its title - and calendar-more for the rest. data-outside quiets a day of another month, data-weekend tints a weekend, aria-current="date" marks today. In a region narrower than 576 px the events fold to their dots. The heading, the previous and next months and the switch of views are the page's links.

Days in a state

A day's data-state tints it: worked, open, pending, absent and off - a time sheet, the days a person can still book. calendar-legend, a list of items with the same data-state, says what each tint means, and every day says its state in words too - in its button's name, or in a visually-hidden word when it is no button; in forced colors the states draw as a line, a dashed and a dotted one. button.calendar-day makes the day's number a button the page opens or picks the day with - its aria-label the whole date and the state, aria-pressed on the day picked.

A date field

date-input around one input type="date" draws it as a field that shows the day and opens a month below it to pick one from; on a window narrower than 576 px the month is a sheet from the bottom of the dimmed page, with a grab bar, the words of the field's label and a Close button the library makes, dragged down by its head to close. The input stays the form's value, an ISO date such as 2026-11-18, with its name, min and max; without the script it is the browser's own date field, and with it the browser's own date picker never opens. The names of the months and the days are the page language's, and the year is the Gregorian one, whatever calendar the phone is set to, unless the page asks for another. A day picked closes the month and gives the focus back to the field. A face reads the day in the page's pattern, or in its language's order without one - 12/02/2026 in English -, and leaves out the year under data-format="short"; under data-weekday the page's Today, Tomorrow or Yesterday comes before the date, else the day's whole name.

The day's name shows first; the free places under each day.

Two inputs make the field a period: one face over both, the start and the end, the month's first pick the start and its second the end. With a mouse, under data-typing, a day is typed in the face itself, in two parts for a period, and a button beside it opens the month: the page's pattern, 21.11 for this year, 211126 run together, a month's name, a pasted ISO date - the up and down arrows step it a day, and a day that reads as none or that cannot be picked is refused in the page's words. Under a finger the face stays the button: a tap opens the month and never the keyboard.

In a tracked form the inputs are one field: data-changed and a refusal's aria-invalid go on the face, and the refusal's words and the focus there too.

A day or a period

date-pick draws the month itself, for the page to put on a screen, in its own sheet or below a column's filter: a day for its one input type="date", a period for its two, the first the start and the second the end. In a period the first pick is a period of that one day at once, and the second its end; a pick before the start starts it again, and the start again keeps the one day. Above the month the choice is in words, a period with its length, and Clear while there is one; the page's quick picks follow, then the bar with the previous and the next month, the lists of the months and the years, and Today when today's month is not shown. Today is tinted and framed, the days before it quiet on the page's grey and still to pick, a day not to pick struck through.

Which days to pick

A box picks only the days its bounds and its rules allow; the others are struck through, say why when the page gives a reason, and stay where the focus can reach them. A day the inputs already hold stays chosen even when the rules leave it out now - a booking made before -, and the library never writes such a day itself.

AttributeWhat it does
min, maxOn the first input, the first and the last day to pick, ISO dates.
data-min, data-maxOn the box, the same counted from today: today, +1 from tomorrow, -30 thirty days back - or an ISO date, for a box of several days whose input is no date. With the input's own, the stricter holds; counted from today, it never goes stale on a page left open past midnight. data-min="today" takes no day in the past, data-max="today" none in the future.
data-enableOn the box, the days that may be picked, when it has the attribute: comma-separated rules, any of which takes a day.
data-disableOn the box, the days that may not, whatever data-enable says: the same rules.
data-min-days, data-max-daysOn a period's box, its fewest and its most days: once its start is picked, an end out of reach is not to pick and says date.shortest or date.longest. With more than one day at least, the first pick leaves the end empty.
data-unbrokenOn a period's box, no day not to pick inside it: once its start is picked, the days after the next such day are not to pick either - a unit rented, a room taken.
data-open-end, data-open-startOn a period's box, its end may stay empty - a leave with no end yet -, or its start too, a filter's "until": the first pick leaves the end empty, the start picked again keeps it so, and a typed part may be cleared.

Several days

data-multiple on a date-pick or a date-input picks separate days for its one input, which holds them as ISO dates with commas between, 2026-12-12,2026-12-27 - the same text a data-enable takes, so the days a tour runs, kept as they came, are the only ones its booking field offers. A pick turns a day on or off; with Shift, every day from the last one turned turns the same way, the ones not to pick left out. The chosen days are a list under the months, each with its remove, and the words above count them; a quick pick adds its days. The grids are aria-multiselectable, and a completed choice - the focus leaving, the month closing, Clear, a remove - fires one change.

PartWhat it does
data-multipleOn the box, several days for its one input, of any type: type="text" keeps a list to type where the script does not run.
date-pick-daysThe chosen days, a list of chips under the months; each removes its day, named by date.remove with the whole date.

The page's settings

How a date reads, where a week starts and when today is belong to the person or the company, not to each field: a page sets them once on <html>, and any element around a box - the box itself - sets them for the boxes inside it. Without them a box reads as before: the page language's order, Monday first, the Gregorian year, today on the device's clock.

<html lang="en" data-date-format="d.m.Y" data-first-day="0" data-time-zone="Asia/Bangkok" data-typing>
AttributeWhat it does
data-date-formatA pattern of PHP's date() letters: d and j the day with and without its zero, m and n the month, Y and y the year, M and F the month's short and whole name, D and l the weekday's; a backslash writes a letter as it is. d.m.Y, m/d/Y, j M Y, M j, Y. Names and digits are the page language's, a month's name as it stands beside a day.
data-first-dayThe first day of a week, 1 for Monday to 7 for Sunday; 0 is Sunday too.
data-calendarbuddhist writes the year 543 ahead, in the faces, the titles and the year list, and reads a typed one back; gregorian keeps a box to the Gregorian year under a page that says otherwise.
data-time-zoneThe zone today is counted in, an IANA name, Asia/Bangkok: today's frame, the past's grey, Today, data-min and data-max. A company's business day, while the person may be elsewhere.
data-weekendThe weekdays the heads tint and a weekend rule takes, 6 7 unless it says; 5 6 for Friday and Saturday.
data-typingDays typed in the face where the pointer is not a finger; data-typing="off" below it keeps a box to its button.

Accessibility

  • Both are tables: a screen reader reads a day with its weekday's header, a cell with its thing and its day. Weekdays are abbr with the full name.
  • A day's number is a time with its date; "+2" says "+2 more" with a hidden word.
  • A busy or over cell says its number; the tint repeats it, and an over cell is also outlined in forced colors.
  • A day's state is in words, never in its tint alone; a day that is a button is named by its whole date and its state, "November 4, waiting for approval", and the picked one says aria-pressed="true".
  • A month to pick from is the WAI-ARIA grid, one grid per month: one day takes the Tab stop and the arrows move it across the months shown, a screen reader enters it as an application and reads each day by its whole date, its count, chosen, today or not available and why. The month and the choice are read politely as they change; several days make the grids aria-multiselectable, and their list says each day again with its remove.
  • The lists of the months and the years are the browser's own selects, named by the page's words: the keyboard, a screen reader and a phone's wheel take them as any select.
  • A typed day is refused with the browser's own message, the page's words; under a finger the field stays a button, so the keyboard never covers the month.
