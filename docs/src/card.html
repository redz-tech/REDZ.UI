Cards

A card is a bordered region of a screen: a header with its title and actions, a body, a footer. It is a region - the width prefixes inside it measure the card, not the window.

Examples

A card that is a section of the screen is a <section> named by its heading. The header holds an icon, the title and whatever belongs to it; the footer holds the card's actions.

A body that scrolls

card-scroll with a height - max-h-*, from 160 to 640 px - keeps the card's header and footer in view and scrolls its body between them, as a dialog does: a list of tickets, a thread above its reply box.

A state on its edge

The utility edge-top-{tone} draws a 3 px line of a state on a card's top edge, instead of coloring its frame; edge-start-{tone} does it on the start edge of a row. The words still say the state.

Accessibility

  • A card that is a part of the page is a <section> with aria-labelledby pointing at its heading; a card in a list of cards is an <article> or a list item.
  • A card has no outer margin: the gap between cards belongs to their parent.
  • A card being refreshed in place turns busy with REDZ.ui.busy(card, promise) - see Busy.
