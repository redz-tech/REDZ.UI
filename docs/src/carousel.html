Carousels

A row of slides seen one or a few at a time: tours, photos, reviews. The browser scrolls and snaps the row - a swipe, a wheel, the arrow keys - and the buttons move a slide back or on.

Examples

carousel on the frame, a named region; carousel-inner on the row, which takes the focus; carousel-item on each slide. Buttons with command="--prev" or "--next" and aria-controls naming the carousel move a slide, round the ends. An empty carousel-indicators fills with a button per slide. The slide in place is aria-current="true".

Several at a time

Three custom properties on the carousel set the row, in the module's own stylesheet: --redz-carousel-items slides in view, --redz-carousel-gap between them and --redz-carousel-peek of the next one at the edges. carousel-center snaps a slide to the middle, carousel-auto lets each slide keep its own width.

.tour-reviews { --redz-carousel-items: 2; --redz-carousel-gap: 12px; --redz-carousel-peek: 24px; }

One at a time, fading, playing

carousel-fade stacks the slides and shows the current one. carousel-overlay lays its buttons over the slides. A button with command="--play" plays the slides on every 5 seconds while it is aria-pressed="true" - written so, it plays from the start - and pauses while the pointer rests on the carousel or the focus is in it; it never plays for a person who asks for less motion.

Keyboard and accessibility

  • The carousel is a named region and each slide a group named by the page's text slide - “2 of 5”; an indicator button says the same, the current one aria-current="true".
  • The row takes the focus (tabindex="0" and a name): the arrow keys scroll it, a slide at a time in a fading carousel.
  • A slide a person moves to by a button is told politely in a status of the carousel; nothing is told while it plays.
  • The play button is a toggle button, pressed while it plays; the pointer resting on the carousel, the focus inside it and a hidden page pause it (WCAG 2.2.2).
  • The buttons have no commandfor, which WebKit would announce as collapsed; their icons mirror right to left. An indicator is a 24 px target round its dot.
