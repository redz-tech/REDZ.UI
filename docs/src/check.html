Checks, radios and switches

The browser's own checkbox and radio, drawn by the library: check, radio and switch on the input itself, form-check on the label around it.

Examples

The class goes on the <input>; the label wraps it, so a click anywhere on the words toggles it.

Included

Radios

Radios with one name are one choice; the arrow keys move it.

Departure

Switches

A switch is a checkbox with role="switch": on or off, applied at once. One that cannot change keeps its focus with aria-disabled="true" and says why in a hint.

Keyboard and accessibility

  • Space toggles a check or a switch; the arrow keys move the choice among radios of one name.
  • A set of checks or radios sits in a <fieldset> whose <legend> names it.
  • The marks are drawn from the browser's own :checked and :indeterminate, and keep showing in forced colors and in print.
  • aria-disabled="true" keeps the control focusable and readable, so its hint can say why; the library ignores clicks and Space on it, as on a button so marked.
