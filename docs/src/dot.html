Dots

An 8 px dot of a tone beside a word: online, live, the color of a site. The dot repeats what the word says; it never says it alone.

Examples

Offline Primary Secondary Live Down Slow Updating

Categories

dot-chart-1 to dot-chart-8 take the charts' eight colors, for a category - a site, a team, a kind of job - not a state: the dot beside a name in a legend or a list matches its series in a chart.

Live

dot-live breathes while its data-state="running" - a job at work, a stream on air - and is still at any other state, and always with reduced motion. The word beside it says the state.

Accessibility

  • A dot has no name; the word beside it carries the meaning. In forced colors every dot is drawn in the text color, so the word is what tells them apart.
  • Dots print with their colors.
