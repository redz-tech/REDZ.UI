Drawers

A modal panel at the end of the screen — or at its start with drawer-start — for filters, a record's details, a long form beside the list it belongs to. Bootstrap 5 calls it offcanvas; it is a <dialog> here.

Examples

A drawer opens and closes like a dialog: command="show-modal" and command="close". It slides in from its side — mirrored right to left — unless the person asks for less motion.

Keyboard and accessibility

  • Everything said of dialogs holds: the focus moves in and back, Tab stays inside, Esc closes.
  • A drawer is a region: the width prefixes inside it measure the drawer, 28 rem wide at most.
  • The drawer's body scrolls by itself; its header stays.
