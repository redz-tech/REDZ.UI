Form fields

Text fields, selects and text areas with Bootstrap 5's names: a label floating in the field or above it, a hint, an error said in words, an icon inside the field, chips for typed values.

Examples

form-floating keeps the label inside the field and lifts it when the field has a value or the focus. The label follows the control, and the control needs a placeholder - it is how CSS knows the field is empty.

A label above, a hint, an error

form-field stacks a field's parts: form-label, the control, form-text for a hint and invalid-feedback for an error. The control points at both with aria-describedby; an error sets aria-invalid="true", and the browser's own :user-invalid draws the same after the person has edited the field.

An icon in the field

form-adorn puts an icon inside a field or a select, at its start; the field keeps its own name. With form-floating on the same element the label starts after the icon, inside the one border - the way a sign-in form draws its fields.

Sizes

form-control-sm and form-select-sm are 28 px high, the default 32 px, -lg 40 px - the same heights as the buttons beside them.

A one-time code

form-control-otp spaces the digits of a code from an app or a message in one field. One field takes a paste and the code the system offers above the keyboard - a box per digit takes neither - and the form goes by its button, never by the last digit.

Spaces and dashes are fine.

A new password

A button with command="--reveal" for a password field shows its text and hides it again - aria-pressed says which - and the field hides it on its form's submit and when the page comes back from the history. Each change bubbles from the field as redz:reveal, detail.revealed true or false, so a page that copies the shown text elsewhere hides it there too. Each element with data-strength naming the field shows its level as it is typed: shorter than its minlength, long enough, then longer or of three kinds of characters, then both - a meter at once, and the words, the page's texts strength.short, .fair, .good and .strong, once the typing pauses, said politely by a live region of the field's own - VoiceOver says nothing of a password field's own description changing, so the words' element needs no aria-live. The level is a hint: the server keeps its own rules.

At least 12 characters. A phrase of a few words is easy to remember.

A range

form-range on an <input type="range"> is the browser's own slider in the theme's accent: it keeps its keys (the arrows, Home, End, Page Up and Page Down) and its look in forced colors. The label says what it sets; when the number matters, write it beside the slider.

A button beside a floating field

btn-float makes a button as tall as a floating field, for the one action beside it; with btn-icon it is square.

A text that grows

data-grow on a textarea starts it at its rows - rows="1" is one line - and grows it with its text, without the five lines a text area takes otherwise. Where the browser has field-sizing: content - Chrome 123, Firefox 152, Safari 26.2 - CSS does it; below, the library sizes it from its text as it is typed, reset and loaded. A max-h-* caps it, and past the cap it scrolls.

A count of characters

form-counter with data-counter naming a field shows how much is typed against its maxlength, or against the counter's data-counter-max, which lets the text go over - a title the search results cut. From nine tenths it turns to the warning tone, past the most to danger (data-state near and over), and once the typing pauses near or over, the count is said politely in the page's text counter. Joined to the field by aria-describedby, the count is read with it.

Number steps

Buttons with command="--step-up" or "--step-down" and aria-controls naming a number field step it by its step within its min and max, and fire input and change as typing does; at an end a button is aria-disabled. The arrow keys in the field step it already: the buttons are for a pointer.

Chips for typed values

chip-input is one field of several typed values, drawn as one box: the values as chips, then the entry, a form-ghost of any type. What the form sends is the box's hidden field - the values joined by data-chips-join, a comma by default - and the chips are drawn from it, at load and whenever it changes. Enter, the separator or the command="--add" button takes what is typed, by the entry's own rules - min, max, step, pattern, type; a value held already and one past data-chips-max are refused too. A refusal keeps the typed text, marks the entry aria-invalid and stands in words under the box. Words left in the entry are taken as the person leaves it for a place outside the box and as the form is sent or saved; a refusal there holds the form, the focus in the entry. A page that saves the form by its own script calls REDZ.ui.settle(form) first and holds its save on false. data-chips-order sorts the values - numbers as numbers in a number entry, words in the page's language - and without it they stay as typed. A chip reads as the entry's datalist option of its value, else as the value.

A colour

form-control-color is the browser's own colour picker in a field's box. A text field with data-color-for naming the picker is its hex twin: each follows the other, and a twin that holds no colour - #rgb or #rrggbb, the # optional - is aria-invalid. A stored colour is drawn by data-color, which the library sets as --redz-color through the CSSOM, so a page under a strict CSP writes no style: on a dot, an icon-tile - its icon black or white by the colour's lightness -, a choice-media, and the edges edge-top-color and edge-start-color.

Overdue

Sent on a change

data-submit="change" on a form or on one of its controls sends the form when such a control changes - a filter's select, a sort, a switch - by requestSubmit, so the browser's checks and the page's submit listeners run. A text field changes when it is left, not at every key; a combobox's list when a value is chosen.

Shown by another field

data-show-when on a region names a field of its form, and data-show-in the values that show it, a space between them; without it, any value does. A checkbox or a radio counts its value when checked. A hidden field beside a checkbox of the same name is what the form sends while it is off: data-show-in can name its value, and a region without data-show-in does not count it. While the region is hidden its fields are disabled, so the form sends none of them; a field the page disabled itself stays disabled. A region inside a hidden region counts as hidden, whatever its own field holds.

Enabled or required by another field

data-enable-when enables its element while a field of its form holds a value data-enable-in lists, and data-require-when with data-require-in makes it required, read as data-show-when reads: the values a space apart, any value without the list. A rule on a field owns its disabled or required, as the server drew it too; a fieldset takes its own disabled; any other element sets each field inside it, and a field the page disabled or required itself stays so. A disabled field counts as empty for every rule, as the form sends nothing of it: the fields of a hidden region drive nothing. A field hidden by a class or by hidden counts by its value, a hidden input too, and a field inside the rule's element never counts.

A disabled field is out of the keyboard's reach, so the field that drives it says why: while a rule disables a field, a text with its own data-show-when that the field names in aria-describedby joins, as long as it shows, the aria-describedby of every control of the driving name. A checked radio a rule disables hands its check to the radio of its group with data-enable-else, which fires input and change; without one the group is left with none checked. A number's step buttons follow the field.

Keyboard and accessibility

  • Every control has a visible label: a <label for>, or aria-label only where the context says it all - a search field with its icon.
  • A placeholder is never the label: it goes away as the person types. In form-floating the placeholder is only the empty marker.
  • An error is said in words in invalid-feedback, joined to the control by aria-describedby; a color alone never tells it.
  • The borders of fields and the focus ring are at least 3:1 against the page in both themes; the text 4.5:1.
  • form-select paints its own chevron from the theme, mirrored right to left; a multiple select or one with size drops it.
  • A count of characters is said only near or over its most, once the typing pauses; at every key it would drown the typing.
  • Number step buttons are named by aria-label and keep their place in the Tab order; from the keyboard the field's own arrow keys step it.
  • A region shown by another field is hidden while it does not apply, so a screen reader skips it and the form sends nothing of it.
  • A field disabled by another field is skipped by Tab: its reason, joined to the driving field, is heard there.
  • In a chips field, Enter, the separator or the --add button takes the entry, and Enter never sends the form. Each chip's remove button keeps its place in the Tab order; a click, Delete or Backspace on it removes the chip, and the focus goes to the next chip, else to the entry. Backspace in the entry only edits its text: a person who clears the text by it never loses a chip.
  • A refused value is said at once, in the words that stand under the box; an added or a removed value is said politely. The remove buttons and the --add button follow the entry's disabled, its fieldset's too.
