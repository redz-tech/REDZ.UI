Input groups

A field joined to what belongs to it - a unit, a prefix, a button, a choice - into one control. The borders meet by themselves; a menu or a hidden element inside the group is left out of the joining.

Examples

input-group around a form-control or a form-select and its parts: input-group-text for words and icons, a btn for an action. An icon inside the field itself is form-adorn.

Keyboard and accessibility

  • Every field of a group has its own name - a <label> or aria-label. The words beside it are not its name: point at a unit with aria-describedby, so it reads as “Adult price, 2,500, THB”.
  • The parts keep their own keys: Tab goes from the field to its button, a menu opens as every menu does.
  • An error goes under the group, in its form-field, never inside it.
