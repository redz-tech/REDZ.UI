Kiosk

A full screen for a tablet that stays on at a door: a name on top, big language buttons, a huge clock while it waits, one round state and one or two huge actions - read from across a room and pressed with a glove.

Example

kiosk fills the window with a column: kiosk-head, kiosk-langs, kiosk-main in the middle - the page's main - and kiosk-foot. It is not selectable and answers a tap at once, without waiting for a double tap. A kiosk is dark: data-theme="dark" on its html, or on the kiosk itself.

The states

kiosk-state on an icon tile is the round state, in the tile's tones: kiosk-state-idle is smaller and breathes while the screen waits (still when motion is reduced), aria-busy="true" turns a ring round it while a tap is checked. kiosk-title and kiosk-line say what happened; a btn-xl in kiosk-main grows to a kiosk's size. kiosk-corner holds a camera's preview, mirrored, at the bottom end.

A kiosk that reads badges through a keyboard-wedge reader takes what it types in a field kept focused offscreen, with inputmode="none" so no keyboard opens.

Accessibility

  • The words carry the state; the tone and the icon repeat them. Say a new state in a polite live region the page keeps - the title is a good one.
  • The language buttons are a named group of btn btn-xl toggles, each with its lang and aria-pressed; the pressed one turns light on dark.
  • The type grows with the screen; nothing is smaller than 14 px, and every button is at least 56 px tall.
