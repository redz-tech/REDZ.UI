List groups

A column of items in one frame - plain rows, or links and buttons to choose from - with the current one and the unavailable ones marked by the same attributes the browser reads.

Examples

list-group on a list, list-group-item on each item.

  • Ang Thong Marine Park
  • Koh Tao and Koh Nang Yuan
  • The Five Islands by kayak

Links and buttons

list-group-item-action makes an item a choice. The current one is aria-current - "page" for a link to the page shown, "true" otherwise; one that cannot be chosen is a disabled button, or a link without href and with aria-disabled="true".

With checks

Reordering

A list with data-reorder naming a hidden field moves its items, each with a data-value, by a drag-handle: a button opening the list's menu of moves, whose items carry data-move - up, down, first, last - or the pointer dragging the handle. A move is done at once, the focus back on the handle, and the page's text moved says the new place. Then redz:move fires on the item: a page that saves cancels it and answers with a promise - refused, the move is undone and the page's words are said; left alone, hidden fields beside the list hold the new order for the form.

  • The pier
  • The emerald lake
  • Lunch on the beach
document.addEventListener('redz:move', (event) => {
	event.preventDefault();
	const {item, parent, position} = event.detail;
	event.detail.respondWith(fetch('/stops/' + item.dataset.value + '/place', {method: 'POST', body: new URLSearchParams({parent: parent?.dataset.value ?? '', position})})
		.then((answer) => answer.ok || Promise.reject('The stop could not be moved')));
});

Keyboard and accessibility

  • Links and buttons keep their own keys: Tab from one to the next, Enter - and Space on a button - to choose. A list of links that moves between pages is a <nav> with its own name.
  • The current item is aria-current, never a class: the screen reader says “current page”, the look follows the attribute.
  • A link that may not be followed has no href: aria-disabled alone would leave it working.
