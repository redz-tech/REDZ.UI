List groups

A column of items in one frame - plain rows, or links and buttons to choose from - with the current one and the unavailable ones marked by the same attributes the browser reads.

Examples

list-group on a list, list-group-item on each item.

Links and buttons

list-group-item-action makes an item a choice. The current one is aria-current - "page" for a link to the page shown, "true" otherwise; one that cannot be chosen is a disabled button, or a link without href and with aria-disabled="true". A plain item whose title is a stretched-link is that link as a whole, as a table's row is: a click anywhere on it but on its own controls follows the title - with Ctrl, Cmd or the middle button into a new tab - unless the person was selecting its words, which stay selectable; the title is the one link a keyboard and a screen reader meet. Its other links, buttons, switches and handles stay their own. A list-group-actions at its end holds the item's buttons; its icon buttons are dimmed until the item is pointed at or holds the focus, on a device with a pointer, while a button with words keeps its contrast.

Flush, numbered, with counts

list-group-flush drops the frame, for a list inside a card: its rows' words line up with the card's. A flush list of list-group-item-action rows keeps the rows' room, so a hover's tint and the current row's bar stand off the words - as in a card that scrolls. list-group-numbered on an <ol> numbers the items.

With checks

Filtered by a search

A search field with data-filter naming a region hides, as the person types, the region's items whose words lack one of the typed words - case and accents aside: list items, items with a data-value, media tiles, tree items and table rows; an item's data-search stands in for its text. A tree keeps the ancestors of a match and opens their groups. An element with data-filter-empty in the region shows when nothing matches. The count is said once the typing pauses, in the page's count text found; Esc in the field clears it. A hidden item carries data-filtered.

Filtered by buttons

A button with command="--filter" and aria-controls naming the region shows only the items whose data-NAME holds a word of its value: NAME is the button's name, state without one, and an item may hold several words - data-state="late failed". The buttons of one name are one choice wherever they stand, their aria-pressed saying which; a button with value="" shows all and stays pressed while nothing narrows, and without one, pressing the pressed button again shows all. Names narrow together, and with the search. A data-filter-count inside a button shows how many items it would show; with the region's id as its value anywhere else it reads the page's text filter.count, "3 of 9". An element with data-filter-group - a card of items, a section - hides while none of its items shows, and a count inside it is its own. A press says the count; the region fires redz:filter with {count, total}; items the page puts in or changes are filtered again. REDZ.ui.filter(region, {state: 'paid'}) chooses from code and answers the count; null clears every choice and the search.

Searched on the server

A field with data-filter-server hides nothing itself: it asks the page by redz:search on the field, with {query, form, signal} - form the FormData of its <search> or form, a status select beside it included. It asks 600 ms after the typing pauses, at once on Enter and on Esc, which clears it, and at once when another control of its search changes. The page cancels the event and answers with a promise by event.detail.respondWith; meanwhile the region carries aria-busy and, in a form-adorn, the field shows a ring at its end. A newer question aborts the signal of the one before, and a late answer is dropped. The count is said in the page's text found: the answer's count, else the items there are. A Show more over the region starts again from the answer.
  • The pier gate does not open
  • Refund for a cancelled trip
  • A second invoice for one booking
document.addEventListener('redz:search', (event) => {
	if (!event.target.matches('[data-filter="requests"]')) {
		return; // a combobox's search fires redz:search too
	}
	event.preventDefault();
	const {form, signal} = event.detail;
	event.detail.respondWith(fetch('/requests?' + new URLSearchParams(form), {signal})
		.then((answer) => answer.json())
		.then(({rows, count}) => {
			document.getElementById('requests').replaceChildren(...rows.map(row));
			return {count};
		}));
});

Show more

A list-more under a list holds its count and a button with command="--more" and aria-controls naming the list - a list group, any element whose children are its items, a table or its body. The button fires redz:more on the list with {shown, signal}; the page answers with a promise by event.detail.respondWith and the button is busy until the page has put the next items in. Then the focus goes to the first new item - its first link or button, else the item - and the page's text more.loaded says how many came. The answer may say {total} or {done: true}; an answer that adds nothing is the end too, and data-more-total on the button is a total known from the start. At the end the button hides. With data-more-max it hides at that many items and a data-more-cap beside it shows the page's sentence instead - "Narrow the search to see older requests". A data-more-count reads the page's text more.count, "10 of 23", or more.some, "10+", while the total is unknown. A refusal keeps the button and the focus where they were.

Reordering

A list with data-reorder naming a hidden field moves its items, each with a data-value, by a drag-handle: a button opening the list's menu of moves, whose items carry data-move - up, down, first, last - or the pointer dragging the handle: a lifted copy follows the pointer, the item's place - an empty slot - goes where it will land, and the other items glide aside; near the edge of the box the list scrolls in, it scrolls, and Escape takes the item back. A move by the menu glides too; with reduced motion nothing glides. Each move ends with the focus back on the handle, and the page's text moved says the new place. Then redz:move fires on the item: a page that saves cancels it and answers with a promise - refused, the move is undone and the page's words are said; left alone, hidden fields beside the list hold the new order for the form.

Keyboard and accessibility

  • Links and buttons keep their own keys: Tab from one to the next, Enter - and Space on a button - to choose. A list of links that moves between pages is a <nav> with its own name.
  • The current item is aria-current, never a class: the screen reader says “current page”, the look follows the attribute.
  • A link that may not be followed has no href: aria-disabled alone would leave it working.
