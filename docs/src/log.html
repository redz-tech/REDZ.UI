Logs and diffs

What a job printed, line by line as it runs, and what changed between two versions of a text - a template, a setting, a translation.

A log

pre class="log" sets a job's output in the monospace font, wraps its long lines and scrolls in its own box once a max-h-* utility caps its height - give it tabindex="0" then, so the keyboard scrolls it too. A line in a span with data-tone - success, warning, danger, info or muted - takes that tone; the words still say what happened. With data-follow the end stays in view while lines come in, as in a terminal, unless the reader scrolled up to read an earlier one; back at the end, it follows again.

09:14:02 Columns: title, price, date, seats, language
09:14:02 Row 1-20 read
09:14:02 Row 21-40 read

A diff

div class="diff" holds one element a line: an ins is a line added, marked "+" and tinted green, a del a line taken away, "-" and red, anything else a line kept. In a kept line, del and ins show the words changed inside it, side by side. Lines of code carry translate="no", so a page's translator leaves them as they are, and a log or a diff of code is dir="ltr" on a right-to-left page; a diff of Arabic words keeps the page's direction.

Accessibility

  • A log that scrolls is focusable and named, so a keyboard reaches it and a screen reader says what it is. A log the person waits on may be role="log", a live region that says new lines politely; one that prints many lines a second says a summary instead.
  • A diff says its changes in its signs and its words, not in its colors: a screen reader reads "+" and "-" before a line, and in forced colors an added word is underlined and a removed one struck through.
