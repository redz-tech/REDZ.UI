Logos

A white mat for someone else's logo - a bank, a payment service, a partner - that keeps it readable in both themes and gives logos of any shape the same weight on a line.

Examples

logo around an img: a white ground with a thin border and rounded corners in both themes and in forced colors, the picture contained in it. One height for a line - 32 px, logo-sm 28 px, logo-lg 40 px, an h-* utility for a banner, h-88 - and a width by the logo's area: data-ratio, its width by its height as the server measured it, widens the mat by the square root of that ratio, from square to four times as wide, so a long wordmark and a round mark look the same size.

A logo drawn on a solid color fills the mat in that color: data-color is the color of its edge, and logo-fade on the picture softens its sides into it. A photo gets a blurred copy of itself behind it, an img class="logo-ground" with an empty alt.

On a choice tile, a choice-media takes the same data-ratio, data-color and logo-ground: the banner of a payment method.

Accessibility

  • A logo that names something has an alt with that name; beside the same name in words, an empty one.
  • The mat stays white in the dark theme and in forced colors, so a logo made for white keeps its colors.
