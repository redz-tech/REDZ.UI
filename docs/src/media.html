Media library

A folder of images as square tiles or as compact rows: checked for actions on many at once, moved by a handle, each with its own actions.

A package of the release

The media library is linked apart, on the screens that show it, beside the two files; its switch of views and its reordering are the main script's.

A folder

media-grid lays media-tiles in as many columns as fit. A tile holds its check of class media-tile-check, a drag-handle when the folder keeps an order, the square media-tile-thumb - cut to fill, so tiles never jump -, media-tile-body with media-tile-name and media-tile-meta, and media-tile-actions. A checked tile is outlined; a tile the search hides is hidden. data-view="list" turns the tiles into rows without previews, the name with its file icon.

The switch is two buttons with command="--view", aria-controls naming the grid and a value: the library puts the value in the grid's data-view, says in aria-pressed which view shows and tells the page in redz:view (detail.view); the library stores nothing - the page that remembers the choice writes data-view when it loads. With data-reorder the tiles move as a list's items do, in two dimensions in the grid. Checked tiles bring up a selection bar.

Choosing

A chooser of images - the cover of a page, the photos of a tour - is a fieldset of tiles, each a label class="media-tile" around its media-tile-check: a radio to pick one, a check to pick some. A click anywhere on the tile picks it, and the focus ring goes round the whole tile; the body is made of spans, as a label holds no blocks. media-grid-compact takes smaller tiles and hides their words from sight - the radio is still named by them. data-cover marks a tile with a star and its own words, for the image a page shows first.

Keyboard and accessibility

  • Each check, handle and action is named by its image's file - "Select lagoon.svg", "Delete: lagoon.svg" - so a list of them still says which.
  • The view buttons are a named group; the one that shows is pressed. A change of view keeps the focus on the button.
  • A thumbnail's alt is the image's own; in rows it is not shown, and the name says the image.
  • A chooser is a fieldset whose legend says what is chosen; each radio or checkbox is named by its tile's words - the image's alt and the file's name, the cover mark too. Arrow keys move among the radios of a chooser of one.
