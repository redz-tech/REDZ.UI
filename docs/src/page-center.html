Centered page

A page without the app shell: the brand, one card and a way on, in a column at the middle of the window - sign-in, registration, a password reset, a second factor, an unlock, an enrolment.

Example

page-center on the page's main stands its children in a column 30rem (480 px) wide at the middle of the window, and the column grows down when they are taller; --redz-page-center-width sets another width. page-center-brand holds the logo, the name, one line and the language links; a card in the column is raised. page-center-corner holds the theme button at the top end of the page: it scrolls with the page, and the column starts below it, so on a phone it never covers the logo or the card.

In a page page-center is the main and the brand's name its h1; here they are a div and an h2 under the page's own.

Accessibility

  • The page is one main; the language links are a nav with a name, each link with its lang, the current one with aria-current="page".
  • The corner comes first in the source so Tab meets it before the form; put it after the form when the form should come first.
  • A logo image has an alt of the name only when the name is not written beside it; otherwise alt="".
