PIN pad

A short number that unlocks a trusted device: dots that fill as it is typed and a keypad for a finger, while the keyboard types as it always does.

Example

pin around one field - type="password", inputmode="numeric", autocomplete="off" and the length in maxlength. The library draws the dots and the keypad: 1 to 9, 0 and delete. The PIN goes when its last digit lands: the pad asks the page in redz:pin with detail.value, and is busy while the page's promise runs. false clears the dots and marks them wrong - the page's alert says why; true leaves the pad as it is while the page goes on. Try 2580.

pad.addEventListener('redz:pin', (event) => {
  event.preventDefault();
  event.detail.respondWith(post({action: 'pin_unlock', pin: event.detail.value}).then((answer) => {
    if (answer.ok) {
      location.assign(answer.redirect);
    }
    return answer.ok;
  }));
});

Any length

A minlength below the maxlength takes any length between them: the dots grow as it is typed, and the keypad gets an Enter key beside its 0. With no page listening the pad submits its form - on Enter, or at the last digit of a fixed length.

Keyboard and accessibility

  • The field is the control: it has the label, takes the digits from any keyboard and is read as a secure field with its length. The dots are drawn for the eyes only.
  • The keypad is for touch: its keys stay out of the Tab order and never take the focus from the field. The delete and Enter keys are named by the page's texts pin.delete and pin.enter.
  • Enter in the field sends a PIN of a length the field takes.
  • Wrong, the dots turn red and shake once; with reduced motion they only turn red. The words of the page's alert are what says it.
