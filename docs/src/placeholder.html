Placeholders

Grey bars in the shape of content that is on its way — the first load of a page or a panel. A region that already has content and reloads it gets an overlay instead: see Busy.

Examples

Each bar is a placeholder sized by a width utility. They pulse slowly, and stand still for people who ask for less motion.

Accessibility

  • Placeholders are empty elements; the region holding them is named "Loading" until the content takes its place.
  • Keep the shapes close to the real content, so the page does not jump when it arrives.
