Popovers

A small panel that opens beside its button and holds more than a label: a title, a few lines of text, a link. A click outside or Esc closes it; the page under it stays usable.

Examples

popover on the panel, popover-header on its title, popover-body on its text. The button opens it with commandfor and command="toggle-popover"; the panel stands below the button, its start edge on the button's, and turns above or across where there is no room.

Keyboard and accessibility

  • The panel comes right after its button in the page, so a screen reader reads it there and Tab from the button moves into it. Opening it moves no focus; Esc closes it and brings focus back to the button.
  • The button says whether the panel is open: the browsers set aria-expanded for a button with commandfor, and js/commands.js sets it in Firefox, which does not.
  • A panel with a title and a link or a field in it is role="dialog", named by its title in aria-labelledby. A panel of words only has no role.
  • Nothing that must be read to use the screen lives only in a popover; a choice that needs an answer is a dialog.
  • js/menu-place.js keeps the panel beside its button where an engine cannot anchor it.
