Progress and meters

The browser's own <progress> for work under way and <meter> for a measure within known limits - a quota, a score. The value is an attribute, the browser announces it, and a progress without a value says the work has no known end.

Examples

progress on a <progress> with value and max; the words of the value go beside the bar, text-nowrap so the bar gives way, and the label names it.

40 % 40 %

Tones and sizes

progress-{tone} for the tones, progress-sm and progress-lg for the height; progress-striped lays stripes on the bar, progress-animated moves them while motion is allowed.

Meters

meter on a <meter>: low, high and optimum divide the range, and the browser colours the value by the part it falls in - green in the good part, amber next to it, red in the far one.

3.1 of 10 GB 3.1 of 10 GB
8 of 10 GB 9.6 of 10 GB

Hairlines

progress-xs, 3 px, and meter-xs, 2 px, sit under a row's words without fighting them: how far a job is between its runs, amber when it is late, and how often it succeeded, colored by the meter's own parts.

Nightly backup next in 2 h 98%
Mail queue late by 6 min 71%

Along the window's top

REDZ.ui.progress(promise) shows a thin bar along the top of the window while a request runs, and answers the promise: it comes after 150 ms, so a quick answer shows nothing, then stays at least 400 ms, so it never flickers; however many requests run at once, there is one bar. It is progress-top, in the top layer, and readers do not hear it - what waits says so itself, a busy region or button. Under reduced motion the bar stands still.

const rows = await REDZ.ui.progress(fetch('/bookings?page=2').then((answer) => answer.json()));

A product sets how much it shows in its tokens: --redz-progress-top-size, --redz-progress-top-track and --redz-progress-top-glow, a box-shadow.

Keyboard and accessibility

  • Name every bar: a <label for>, aria-label or aria-labelledby. The browser announces the value as a percentage of max; the words beside the bar are for the eye.
  • Colour is not the message: a meter in the red part also says so in words where it matters.
  • A screen reader does not follow a bar as it fills: say the end of a long task in a notice, by REDZ.ui.toast.
  • In forced colours the bar takes the system's highlight; right to left it fills from the right.
