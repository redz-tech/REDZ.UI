Ratings

Stars that show a mark out of five - an average of reviews, a guide's score - and five radios drawn as stars for giving one.

Examples

rating draws five stars filled to data-v, the mark's share of the most from 0 to 1 - 4.2 of 5 is 0.84; the library sets it as --v through the CSSOM, so a page under a strict CSP writes no style. The stars are a picture: role="img" with the mark in aria-label, and the number in words beside them when it matters. They take the size of the text around them.

4.2 128 reviews

Giving a mark

fieldset class="rating-input" holds five radios, each an input before its label: the library hides the inputs and draws each label as a star, filled up to the one checked - and up to the one under the pointer. The words of each label are for a screen reader, in a visually-hidden span; the legend names the mark. The radios stay the control: the arrow keys move the mark, the form sends it.

Keyboard and accessibility

  • A shown mark is one picture with its words - "4.2 of 5" - not five stars read one by one.
  • A mark given is a group of radios: Tab into it, the arrows move the mark, a screen reader says "4 stars, radio button, checked, 4 of 5".
  • The stars are drawn in the warning tone on the strong border's grey; in forced colors, in the text colour on grey.
