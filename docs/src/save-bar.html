Save bar

The bar at the foot of a form that says what is not saved yet and offers to save or discard it. It sticks to the bottom of the screen while the form is longer than the screen.

Examples

At rest the bar says everything is saved; with unsaved changes it turns save-bar-dirty and names them.

All changes saved
2 unsaved changes: product name, accent color

Accessibility

  • The bar is a region named after what it holds; its words name the changes, not only their count.
  • It does not print, and it never hides the last field of the form: the form keeps room under it.
