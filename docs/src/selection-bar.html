Selection bar

The bar over a list that shows when rows are checked: how many, and what can be done with all of them at once.

Examples

Plain buttons in the bar take its tint; the one suggested action stays btn-subtle-primary, and clearing the selection is a quiet icon button at the end.

2 selected

Accessibility

  • The bar is a region named after the list; its count says what is selected.
  • An action that removes asks first — its words end in “…” and it opens a confirmation.
