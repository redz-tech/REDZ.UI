Stat tiles

A number with its label and an icon in a tone - and, as a button, the filter for what it counts: the numbers a person reads are the buttons they press.

Examples

stat holds an icon tile, a stat-value and a stat-label; stat-primary, stat-success, stat-warning, stat-danger, stat-info and stat-secondary - a quiet zero - tint the icon and the number. As a button with aria-pressed a tile is a toggle: pressed, it wears its tone's border and the list below shows only what it counts. A row is a grid: two tiles a row on a phone, four from md:.

Without a button it only reads: a div class="stat".

Accessibility

  • A tile button's name is its number and its label, "2 Late"; aria-pressed says it filters. Put the row in a group with a name.
  • The tone repeats the label; it never says alone what the tile counts.
  • Pressed, the tile also draws an outline in forced colors.
