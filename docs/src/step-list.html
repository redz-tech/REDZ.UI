Steps and timelines

The steps of a long job as it runs - a release, a publish, an install - each with what it found, and the events of a record's history, one under the other.

A job's steps

step-list is an ol of steps, each with an icon, its name and a step-list-detail at the end; data-state says where it is: done, current - its icon turns while motion is allowed, failed, kept - failed with the earlier state kept - and pending. A job's panel is a card with sticky-top, under the app header while the page scrolls, its head a dot, a title and the time, and a progress.

Accessibility

  • The words carry the state; the icon and the color repeat them. Mark the current step aria-current="step".
  • A job that runs while the person waits says its new step in a polite live region the page keeps - one line per step, not every tick of the clock.
  • A timeline's times are <time datetime>; its details are a description list, read as pairs.
