Steppers

The steps of a task that takes more than one screen: a booking, an import, the first run of a site. A list of steps in order, the one the person is on marked by the attribute a screen reader says, the ones before it done by their place.

Examples

stepper on an <ol>, stepper-item on each step, and aria-current="step" on the step the person is on. Every step before it reads as done, every step after it as still to come; the numbers are counted by the list.

Across

stepper-horizontal sets the steps in a row, each under its number. With a prefix, md:stepper-horizontal, the row starts where the region is wide enough, and the steps stay a column below it.

  1. Upload the file
  2. Match the columns
  3. Check the rows
  4. Import

Steps to go back to

A step done may lead back to its screen: its words are a link inside the stepper-item. A step still to come is plain text, as it cannot be reached yet.

Keyboard and accessibility

  • The list is an <ol> with a name of its own (aria-label): a screen reader says how many steps there are and which one this is.
  • The step the person is on is aria-current="step", never a class: VoiceOver and NVDA say "current step", and the look follows the attribute.
  • The stepper shows where the person is; it moves nothing by itself. The screen's own buttons, "Back" and "Next", move between steps.
  • A step done that leads back is a link, reached by Tab; a step still to come is text, never a disabled link.
