Summaries and totals

The facts of one record in pairs - a label and its value - and the sums at the foot of an invoice or a booking.

Examples

summary on a <dl>: the terms in a column as wide as the longest, the values beside them; in a region narrower than 30rem each value goes under its term.

Booking 24-0187

Excursion
Ang Thong: marine park by speedboat
Date
14 December 2026, 08:30
Guests
2 adults, 1 child
Pickup
Lamai beach, the hotel's lobby

Totals

totals on a <dl> of rows - a <div> each, its label and its value - the numbers at the end in tabular-nums; totals-grand on the last row draws it larger, under a line.

2 adults × 2,500 THB
5,000 THB
1 child × 1,500 THB
1,500 THB
Discount, early booking
-650 THB
To pay
5,850 THB

In a confirmation

REDZ.ui.confirm({rows: [{label, value}]}) shows the same pairs in its dialog - what a payment is, what a move changes - after its note and before its lists; every word is text, never HTML.

Accessibility

  • A description list tells a screen reader which value belongs to which term, so no heading is needed for each pair.
  • A sum written with its sign - "-650 THB" - says a discount without color.
