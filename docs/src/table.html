Tables

A list of records: a title that links to the record, a line under it, a note when something is wrong, a column of checks for batch actions and the row's own actions at the end.

Examples

table on the <table>; table-hover lights the row under the pointer. The head stays at the top of the screen as the page scrolls. Columns that only help on a wide screen hide below md of their card.

States

A row's tone marks what needs attention: table-danger failed, table-warning needs a look, table-secondary is out of use. On a pointer screen the row's actions show on hover and on focus; on a touch screen they always show.

Keyboard and accessibility

  • The row's title is a link; its actions are buttons named with the record — "Edit: Ang Thong", not a column of identical "Edit".
  • The column of actions has a hidden header, and each check its own name: "Select: Ang Thong".
  • A tone is never the only sign: a failed row also says why in its table-note, with an icon.
  • Numbers sit at the end of their cells with tabular-nums, so their digits line up.
  • A table being reloaded turns busy on its card's body: REDZ.ui.busy(body, promise) — not on the <table>, which cannot hold an overlay.
