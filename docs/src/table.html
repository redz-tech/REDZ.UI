Tables

A list of records: a title that links to the record, a line under it, a note when something is wrong, a column of checks for batch actions and the row's own actions at the end.

Examples

table on the <table>; table-hover lights the row under the pointer. The head stays at the top of the screen as the page scrolls. Columns that only help on a wide screen hide below md of their card.

Stripes, borders and groups

table-striped shades every other row, table-striped-columns every other column - under the hover's light and a row's tone alike; table-group-divider on a tbody draws a stronger line above it. table-bordered draws the lines between columns and, outside a card, the table's own frame; table-borderless takes the lines between rows away.

Compact rows

data-density="compact" tightens the rows and the controls of the element it is on and of everything inside it: a table, the card that holds it, a region, or the whole page on <html>. A compact table can stand beside a normal one on the same screen. Bootstrap 5 calls it table-sm.

Groups and subtotals

A row table-group heads the rows under it: one th scope="rowgroup" across the table, a band that does not stick, with an end slot table-group-end for its count or sum. A row table-subtotal closes a group in bold under a line.

Wider than its card

A table with more columns than its card has room for goes in a table-scroll: it scrolls sideways inside the card, and table-scroll-first holds its first column in place. Where the browser lets an element scroll on one axis alone - Chrome 156 on - the head still sticks to the page as it scrolls down; elsewhere the head scrolls away with the page and the rest works the same. The wrapper is a named region with tabindex="0", so a keyboard can scroll it.

The current, the chosen and the running row

A row with aria-current - the record open beside the list - carries the accent's mark at its start; a row with aria-selected="true", chosen in a grid of rows, is tinted; table-active, Bootstrap's name, marks the row that is running now.

A whole row as a link

The row's title link with Bootstrap's stretched-link makes the whole row a link: a click anywhere on it but on its own controls and their cells (table-check, table-actions) follows the title - with Ctrl, Cmd or the middle button into a new tab - unless the person was selecting its text. The title stays the one link a keyboard and a screen reader meet. The library does it by script, as Safari gives a positioned row no box for the link's cover.

Keyboard and accessibility

  • The row's title is a link; its actions are buttons named with the record - "Edit: Ang Thong", not a column of identical "Edit".
  • The column of actions has a hidden header, and each check its own name: "Select: Ang Thong".
  • A tone is never the only sign: a failed row also says why in its table-note, with an icon.
  • Numbers sit at the end of their cells with tabular-nums, so their digits line up.
  • A table being reloaded turns busy on its card's body: REDZ.ui.busy(body, promise) - not on the <table>, which cannot hold an overlay.
