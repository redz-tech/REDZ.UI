Ticking times

A time that keeps itself current: how long a job has been running, how long ago a message came. The page writes the moment; the library writes the words and keeps them right.

Examples

A time whose datetime is the moment - ISO, with its zone. With data-elapsed it shows the time since then as a clock, m:ss under an hour and h:mm:ss after; with data-ago it says how long ago it was, or how soon it comes, in the language of its nearest lang, and its title holds the full moment with the reader's time zone; data-ago="short" says it short, for a narrow place such as a stat tile. One ticker serves every one on the page: each second while an elapsed time is there, else every 30 seconds, and none while the page is hidden. The page's own words stand inside until the library starts.

The backup has been running for 1:35.

The last message came 5 minutes ago; the next tour leaves in 2 hours.

Last backup 42 min. ago.

The words follow the nearest lang, not the page's: a sentence the page keeps in another language - a text not yet translated - has its time in that language too.

Die letzte Sicherung lief vor 1 Stunde.

The reader's own clock

A moment the server knows in UTC, shown by the reader's clock: data-local writes its time, data-local="date" its date and time, and data-local="auto" the time when it falls on the reader's today, the weekday and time in the six days ahead, else the date and time. The hours are written as the language writes them - 07:00 in Russian, 7:00 AM in American English - and the title holds the full moment with the reader's time zone. The server's own text, its UTC time, stands without the script.

The daily limit starts again at 00:00 UTC, your time.

The report was sent Nov 4, 2026, 08:00 UTC.

AttributeWhat it does
data-elapsedThe time since the moment as a clock, m:ss or h:mm:ss, each second.
data-ago, data-ago="short"How long ago, or how soon, in words.
data-local, data-local="date", data-local="auto"The moment by the reader's clock: the time, the date and time, or as much as its distance from today needs.

One of the three on a time; given more, data-elapsed wins, then data-ago. A title the page writes itself stays.

Accessibility

  • A ticking time is never read out as it ticks: a reader hears it where it is, as it is at that moment.
  • Say a wait that matters in words once, where it starts - the time beside it is for the eye.
