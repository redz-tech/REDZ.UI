Toasts

A notice after an action — saved, failed, moved with a way to undo — shown by REDZ.ui.toast() in a stack at the corner of the screen and read aloud from a live region.

Examples

Success and info leave after 8 seconds, unless the pointer or the focus rests on them. An error, and a notice with an action, stay until closed.

The call takes the words and the tone and answers a function that closes the toast:

const close = REDZ.ui.toast({title: 'Moved to the archive', tone: 'info', action: {label: 'Undo', run: restore}});

The stack

The page carries the stack from the start, so that screen readers know its live regions before anything is said in them: role="status" for notices, role="alert" for errors. Without one, the library adds it at load.

<div class="toast-container"><div role="status"></div><div role="alert"></div></div>

Keyboard and accessibility

  • A toast never takes the focus; its close button and its action are in the tab order at the end of the page.
  • While a modal dialog is open, toasts show inside it, since everything outside it is inert.
  • The close button's name comes from the page's texts: close in the island #redz-ui-text.
  • Nothing a person must act on lives only in a toast that leaves by itself.
