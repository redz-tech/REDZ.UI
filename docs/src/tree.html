Trees

Pages under pages, a menu's items, folders: a list whose items open their own lists, each a link or a name, and moved by a handle.

Examples

tree on the list, tree-item on each item, its tree-row holding a tree-toggle - a --toggle button naming the item's tree-group in aria-controls, as on collapse - and the item's link. The groups close as hidden="until-found", so the browser's find in page still opens them. A button with command="--expand-all" or "--collapse-all" for the tree opens or closes every group that holds items.

    Children from the server

    A group with data-lazy and no items fires redz:expand on it when it first opens. A page that loads the items cancels it and answers with a promise: the toggle is busy until it settles, and a refusal closes the group again for another try. The Archive above answers after 400 ms.

    document.addEventListener('redz:expand', (event) => {
	event.preventDefault();
	event.detail.respondWith(fetch('/pages?parent=' + event.target.dataset.parent)
		.then((answer) => answer.text())
		.then((items) => event.target.insertAdjacentHTML('beforeend', items)));
});

    Moved by a handle

    A tree with data-reorder moves its items as a list does - see reordering - and its menu of moves adds data-move="in", to the end of the group of the item above, and "out", after its parent; an item can go in where the item above has a tree-group, empty or not. data-parent names the hidden field of each item's parent, its value the item's key.

          The current page, actions and checks

          A row whose link has aria-current - the page open now - is tinted and its link bold. tree-actions at the end of a row holds its buttons: quiet until the row is hovered or holds the focus on a pointer screen, always shown on a touch screen. A check in a row posts by its own name, for a batch over the chosen pages. A search with data-filter keeps the parents of a match.

          Keyboard and accessibility

          • The tree is a list of lists with buttons and links - the disclosure pattern: Tab goes from one control to the next, Enter or Space opens or closes a group, and the toggle says it in aria-expanded. It is not the ARIA tree widget, which the APG itself keeps for choosing, not for links.
          • A toggle is named after its item; its chevron points along the line of reading, mirrored right to left.
          • The moves are a menu on the handle, so a keyboard, a screen reader and a single tap move an item as a drag does; after a move the focus stays on the moved item's handle and its new place is said politely.
