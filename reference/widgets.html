Widgets

The behavior REDZ.UI adds to the browser's own: menus and tabs by keyboard, a dialog closed by a click outside it, notices, a confirmation and a busy button.

Menu

The arrow keys move through the items, Home and End go to the ends, a letter to the next item it starts; Tab and Escape close it.
